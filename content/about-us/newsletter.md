*Carpentries Clippings* is the quarterly newsletter of The Carpentries. Started in September 2016 as a monthly newsletter, it shifted to a fortnightly publication in August 2017, and then reverted to a monthly schedule in April 2023 until August 2026. It contains updates from the Core Team about programmatic and other developments, a Gratitude of the Month, highlights from our blog, opportunity postings relevant to our community, and more.

## Tax Considerations for Our Global Community

**For US donors:** Beginning with the 2026 tax year, eligible taxpayers who claim the standard deduction can deduct qualifying monetary charitable contributions of up to **$1,000 for individual filers or $2,000 for married couples filing jointly.** No itemising is required.

Your direct donation to The Carpentries may qualify for this new deduction. For additional information, consult the [IRS guidance on charitable contributions](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc506).

**For international donors:** Tax benefits for charitable contributions vary by country. We encourage donors to consult their local tax authority or tax adviser regarding any applicable benefits.

Regardless of where you are in the world, your generosity helps sustain our global community.
